Equipment and technical notes

Equipment legend

The floor has an 8 mm rubber mat with representative grip ribs. Thickness is a preliminary design assumption. The 100 cm crate height includes the floor and forklift runners. The main clear gap between side slats is 10 cm; posts and braces locally intersect the openings. The PMC pallet is represented with outer dimensions of 317.5 × 243.8 cm, a 4 mm sheet and approximately 2.8 cm high edge rails. When centred, the crate is 8.75 cm from each pallet end and 9.4 cm from each side, measured to the outer perimeter; these are not usable loading clearances after deducting the rails. Pallet geometry and attachment details are illustrative. Plastic sheeting runs under the runners and turns up 30 cm on all four sides. Orange: three straps attached to opposing long edges; blue: three straps attached to opposing short edges. Strap widths and net mesh sizes are illustrative. Attachments are positioned on the pallet edge tracks. This visual is not a restraint calculation or an approval of strength or aircraft loading suitability. Sources: VRR, SATCO, PalNet. Timber sections and connections are illustrative. This concept does not replace approval for manufacture, animal welfare or aircraft loading.
